An off-grid, solar-powered charging kiosk

CampusVolt harvests sunlight through a 450W rooftop solar panel, stores it in an on-site 12V 100Ah LiFePO4 battery bank, and shares it across six charging channels. It keeps working even when the campus grid doesn't — no outlet, no problem.

How it works

  • 1. Sunlight in

    Solar panels convert sunlight into DC power throughout the day.

  • 2. Stored safely

    An MPPT controller stores the energy in the LiFePO4 battery bank.

  • 3. Shared intelligently

    Power is balanced across all ports based on demand and battery level.

  • 4. Your device charges

    Plug into any open port and charge — the station handles the rest.
